Privacy policy for Lanternline

Last updated 22 November 2026.

In short: Lanternline does not collect any data. The game does not send anything to Ninna AS or to anyone else.

Who we are

Lanternline is made by Ninna AS, organisation number 916 872 208, Norway. Contact: kristian@ninna.no.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves the following on your device only:

  • your progress in the game (coins, upgrades, catches, journal, play time and best time),
  • your settings (sound on or off, and the volume of music and effects).

The game has no internet access and never transmits this information. Ninna AS cannot see it. It stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android like any other app data. The app has opted out of Android's cloud backup. If you move to a new phone, some phones' own device-to-device transfer may still copy app data; that copy goes from your old phone to your new one, not to us.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for the network permission, or for any other permission.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • It has no accounts, no chat, no leaderboards and no purchases.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Keeping and deleting data

The save stays on your device until you remove it. Deleting the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes everything it has saved. "New game" in the game replaces the saved progress. Since nothing is stored outside your device, Ninna AS holds no data about you and has nothing to delete.

Children

Lanternline is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we hold nothing about anyone, whatever their age.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions

Email kristian@ninna.no. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
