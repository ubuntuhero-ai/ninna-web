Common questions

How do I turn the music off?

Open the grown-up menu and switch Music off.

Can the game go faster or slower?

Yes. Open the grown-up menu and choose the speed. With Auto, the speed for older players goes up a little after several right answers and down again after mistakes.

How do I quit a flight?

Tap the pause button in the top-left corner and choose Quit. Progress is saved first.

How do I delete all data?

Open the grown-up menu and choose Delete all, or delete the app. All data is stored only on the device; see the privacy policy.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Fly Figures works fully offline and never connects to the internet.
