Fly Figures

A calm counting and maths flying game for children aged 5–13.

Fly a little plane over islands, count the figures you see and fly through the right answer. Tasks grow with the child: counting for the youngest, sums and thinking tasks for older players. Each child has their own profile with their own best score.

Made for children

  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection needed, and the game never connects to the internet.
  • No accounts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Settings and deleting data sit behind a grown-up gate.

Coming to the App Store and Google Play.
