Common questions

How do I steer?

Drag a finger anywhere on the screen. Your plane follows the finger and fires by itself.

How do I hijack an enemy plane?

Damage it until an orange ring appears around it, then fly into the ring. The plane joins your squadron, adds its weapon to yours and counts as an extra life.

How do I use the EMP?

When the EMP meter is full, tap briefly with a second finger. All enemy bullets on screen vanish.

How do I change the language, sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the start screen or from the pause menu.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

The first two sectors are free. One purchase unlocks the full game: all five landscapes and bosses, with runs that last as long as you survive. There are no ads and no subscription.

I bought the full game, but it is locked

Open Unlock the full game on the start screen and choose Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store or Google Play with the account that made the purchase.

Where is my high score list stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere; see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes the list.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Iron Aurora plays fully offline. Only the purchase itself goes through the App Store or Google Play.
