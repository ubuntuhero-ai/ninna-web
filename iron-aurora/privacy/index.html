Privacy policy for Iron Aurora

Last updated 11 November 2026.

In short: Iron Aurora does not collect any data. Ninna AS does not receive, see or store anything about the people who play.

Who we are

Iron Aurora is made by Ninna AS, organisation number 916 872 208, Norway. Contact: kristian@ninna.no.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following only on the device:

  • the names typed in on the local high score list, with scores, times and dates,
  • your best scores,
  • settings such as language, sound and vibration,
  • whether the full game has been unlocked.

This information never leaves the device. It is not sent to Ninna AS or to anyone else. The app's data may be included in your own device backup if you have turned that on, like other app data. Ninna AS has no access to any backups.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no internet connections of its own.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It has no accounts, no chat and no online leaderboards.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Purchases

The first two sectors are free. The full game is unlocked with a single in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. Ninna AS does not learn who you are or how you paid.

Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes all its data from the device. A purchase can be restored afterwards with Restore purchase in the game.

Children

Iron Aurora is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we hold nothing about anyone, whatever their age.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions

Email kristian@ninna.no. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
